Security

Encryption that actually means something

No marketing. Just real cryptography, on-device.

01

ECDH key exchange

Your device generates a P-256 keypair. The private half is encrypted with your password and never leaves your phone in plaintext.

02

AES-256-GCM

Every message uses a fresh IV and the shared secret derived via HKDF. Same standard used by banks and governments.

03

Password-wrapped key

PBKDF2 with 600,000 iterations. Even if someone steals your device data, they can't brute-force your key.

04

Zero server knowledge

The server only stores ciphertext and metadata. It can never read your messages, ever.
